<h2>v2.3.4 - Vehicle Anti-Cheat Whitelist Mixin Apply Fix</h2>

<h3>Fix</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Vehicle anti-cheat whitelist mixin failed to apply on 26.2</strong>: the 2.3.3 rework redirected <code>isSingleplayerOwner()</code> inside <code>handleMoveVehicle</code>, but also declared a <code>@Shadow</code> for it. In 26.2 that method was moved from <code>ServerGamePacketListenerImpl</code> up to its superclass <code>ServerCommonPacketListenerImpl</code>, and Mixin cannot resolve a <code>@Shadow</code> against an unregistered superclass, so applying the mixin failed with <code>InvalidMixinException: @Shadow method isSingleplayerOwner()Z was not located in the target class</code> and the whitelist was silently disabled at startup.</li>
<li>Replaced the <code>@Shadow</code> with an <code>@Invoker</code> mixin on <code>ServerCommonPacketListenerImpl</code> (<code>InvokerServerCommonPacketListenerImpl</code>, registered in the <code>server</code> section of <code>utility_core_fixes.mixins.json</code>); the redirect handler now falls back through that invoker. The scoped <code>@Redirect</code> on the single <code>isSingleplayerOwner()</code> call inside <code>handleMoveVehicle</code> is unchanged, so only whitelisted root vehicles skip the <code>moved too quickly</code> check and the other call sites are untouched.</li>
</ul>
