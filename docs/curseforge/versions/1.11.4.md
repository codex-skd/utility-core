<h2>v1.11.4 - Recipe Selector Click Fix</h2>

<h3>Fix</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Recipe selector not responding to mouse clicks</strong>: Although the selector grid was now visible and stable, clicking on recipe variants wasn't being registered. The click detection relied on the <code>hovering</code> flag from the previous render frame, causing a race condition where clicks would miss if the selector wasn't actively hovered during the last render. The selector now performs direct bounds checking on mouse click events, immediately registering any click within a recipe grid cell regardless of the previous render state.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Technical Changes</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>PolymorphClientHandler.onMousePressed()</code> &mdash; refactored to perform direct hit-testing instead of relying on the cached <code>hovering</code> flag. Now calculates whether the click is within selector bounds (X, Y, and grid dimensions) in the event handler itself, ensuring clicks are registered immediately when they occur.</li>
<li>Removed dependency on timing between render cycles and input events, preventing frame-to-frame desynchronization.</li>
</ul>
